# FinWise User Guide

FinWise helps you see exactly how much money your business makes, where it goes, and what stock you have — from your phone or computer.

---

## 1. Getting started

### Create your account (business owner)
1. Open FinWise and tap **Create an account**.
2. Enter your **business name**, choose your **currency** (e.g. ZMW — Zambian Kwacha) and type of business.
3. Enter your name, email and a password (8+ characters).
4. You land on your **Dashboard**. FinWise has already created common income and expense categories for you.

### Install the app on your phone
FinWise is a web app you can install — no app store needed.
- **Android (Chrome):** tap menu **⋮ → Install app** (or *Add to Home screen*).
- **iPhone (Safari):** tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.
- **Computer (Chrome / Edge):** click the install icon in the address bar, or **Settings → App & data → Install app**.

It then opens full-screen like any other app.

### Recommended first steps
1. **Settings → Business**: add your phone, address, tax number (TPIN) and a receipt message.
2. **Inventory → Add product** for each item you sell (see section 4).
3. **Settings → Team**: add your shop assistants (see section 7).

## 2. The dashboard (owners)

| Tile | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Income** | All money received in the period (sales + other income). |
| **Expenses** | All money paid out, including stock purchases. |
| **Net profit** | What you actually earned: income − cost of the goods you sold − running costs. |
| **Stock value** | What your current stock cost you. |

The small arrows compare with the previous period of the same length (green = good). Use the period selector (Today, Last 7 days, This month…) to change the view.

Below the tiles you'll find: the income-vs-expenses chart (tap **Show table** for exact numbers), sales performance, **Where money went**, **Top products**, recent activity and a **Low stock** list. A yellow banner warns you when products need restocking.

## 3. Recording income and expenses

Use this for anything that is **not** a product sale: rent, electricity, salaries, transport, airtime, service income, etc.

1. Go to **Income & expenses** (or use the buttons on the dashboard).
2. Tap **Expense** or **Income**.
3. Enter the amount, choose a category, the date, a short description, and how it was paid (cash, mobile money, bank…). Add a reference number if you have one.
4. Tap **Save**.

**Search & filter** by text, type, category, payment method or date range. The totals at the top update to match your filters.

**Edit / delete** (owners only): use the pencil or bin icons. Entries created automatically by sales or stock purchases can't be edited here — void the sale or adjust stock instead.

### No network? Keep working
If you lose connection, you can still record income and expenses. They're saved on your device and a badge shows **Offline · 1 pending**. When the connection returns they upload automatically. *(Sales need a connection so that stock counts stay correct across all devices.)*

## 4. Inventory

### Add a product
**Inventory → Add product**, then fill in:
- **Name**, **category** (e.g. Beverages) and **unit** (pcs, kg, bag, litre…).
- **Cost price** — what you pay the supplier. **Selling price** — what customers pay. FinWise shows your margin and profit per unit as you type.
- **Opening stock** — how many you have now.
- **Low-stock alert at** — FinWise warns you when stock falls to this level.

### Receive new stock
Tap the **box icon** (📦+) next to a product → **Receive stock**:
- Enter the quantity and cost per unit.
- Leave **Record as expense** ticked so the purchase appears in your expenses.
- FinWise updates the product's average cost automatically.

### Write off or correct stock
Same box icon → **Adjust / write off**. Choose *Remove* for damaged, expired, stolen or own-use items, or *Add* to correct a miscount. Always add a reason.

### Stock history
Tap a product's name to see every movement — opening stock, deliveries, each sale, adjustments — with who did it and the balance after.

## 5. Making a sale

1. Tap **Sell** (bottom bar on phones) or **New sale**.
2. Tap products to add them — tap again to add more. Search by name or code.
3. Adjust quantities in the cart with **−** / **+**.
4. Choose the payment method, add a discount or customer name if needed. *(Credit sales require a customer name.)*
5. Tap **Complete sale**.

FinWise then automatically: reduces stock, records the income, and creates a numbered receipt. You can't sell more than you have in stock.

### Receipts
After the sale tap **View receipt**. From there you can **Print** or **Share** (sends the receipt text to WhatsApp, SMS, etc.).

### Voiding a sale (owners)
Open the sale from **Sales history → Void**, enter a reason. Items go back into stock and the income is removed. Voided sales stay visible (crossed out) for your records.

## 6. Reports (owners)

Go to **Reports** and pick a period or custom dates.

- **Profit & loss** — sales, cost of goods sold, gross profit, each running cost, and **net profit**.
  *Why aren't stock purchases listed as expenses here?* Because you haven't "used up" that money until the goods are sold. Their cost appears as *cost of goods sold* when each item sells. This gives you your true profit.
- **Cash flow** — every shilling actually received and paid out, including stock purchases. Use this to understand your cash position.
- **Expenses / Income by category**, **Product performance** (quantity, revenue, profit, margin per product), **Inventory position**.

**Export:** download Transactions, Sales or Inventory as CSV files (open in Excel or Google Sheets). **Print / PDF** prints a clean copy of the report — choose *Save as PDF* in the print dialog to keep a file.

## 7. Team members

**Settings → Team → Add member.** Enter their name, email and role. FinWise suggests a temporary password; share the sign-in details shown (tap **Copy details**). They'll be asked to choose their own password at first sign-in.

| Can… | Owner | Staff |
|---|:-:|:-:|
| Make sales, view own sales | ✅ | ✅ |
| Record income & expenses | ✅ | ✅ (sees only their own) |
| View stock levels | ✅ | ✅ |
| Add/edit products, receive or adjust stock | ✅ | – |
| Change prices at checkout, void sales | ✅ | – |
| Edit/delete transactions | ✅ | – |
| Dashboard, reports, profits, exports | ✅ | – |
| Business settings, categories, team | ✅ | – |

**Reset password** or **Deactivate** a member at any time. Deactivated staff are signed out immediately; their past sales stay in your records.

## 8. Settings

- **My account** — change your password.
- **Business** — name, currency, contact details, tax number, receipt footer, default low-stock level. *(Changing currency only changes the symbol — amounts aren't converted.)*
- **Categories** — add your own income/expense categories with colours. Categories already used are archived rather than deleted. *Sales* and *Inventory purchases* are automatic and can't be removed.
- **App & data** — install the app, check offline status, export all your data.

---

## 9. Tips & FAQ

- **Record every expense the same day**, even small ones like airtime and transport — they add up.
- **Count your stock monthly** and use *Adjust* to correct differences; frequent write-offs may point to theft or spoilage.
- **Check Product performance** — high-selling items with low margins may need a price review.
- **Signing out on a shared phone** clears cached data. If you have unsynced offline entries FinWise warns you first.
- **Forgot password?** Ask the business owner to reset it in Settings → Team. *(Owners: contact your system administrator.)*
